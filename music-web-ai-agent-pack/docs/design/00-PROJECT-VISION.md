# Project Vision

## Vision
Create a browser-based music creation environment where users can create music, compose melodies, play virtual instruments, arrange tracks, record ideas, and receive AI-assisted recommendations.

The platform should serve both experienced users and beginners who do not know music theory.

## Core Problem
Traditional DAWs can expose technical concepts before a beginner has even formed a musical idea. This project reduces that barrier by allowing the user to start from:
- an instrument,
- a simple musical idea,
- a hummed/sung melody,
- a mood or feeling,
- or an AI suggestion.

## Product Principle
> Users do not need to know music theory to begin creating music. They only need to recognize what sounds good to them.

## Product Identity
The product is NOT only:
- a prompt-to-song generator,
- a static sample player,
- or a virtual piano website.

It is intended to become a **web-native music studio with an AI Music Copilot**.

## Confirmed Pillars
1. Browser Music Studio
2. Virtual Instruments
3. AI-Assisted Music Creation
4. Instrument/Music Suggestions
5. Beginner-Friendly Experience
6. User-Controlled AI Suggestions

## Proposed Differentiators
- Hum/Sing → Melody
- AI Continue My Melody
- AI Build Around My Melody
- AI Preference Learning
- Similar Sound Recommendation
- Natural-Language Music Editing
- Beginner/Advanced modes
- AI Arrangement Assistant
- AI Mixing Assistant

## Suggested MVP Direction
Subject to owner approval:
- authentication/account,
- project management,
- browser studio,
- multi-track editing,
- several virtual instruments,
- basic recording,
- hum/sing recording,
- melody extraction,
- AI melody assistance,
- AI instrument recommendation,
- preview/accept/reject AI suggestions,
- saving,
- basic export.
